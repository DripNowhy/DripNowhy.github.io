# Yi Ding — personal homepage

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors discovering Yi's work, fellow technical creators, research collaborators,
and people following links to his papers and projects.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Yi as a technical creator with taste and a sense of fun, with an
accessible, accurate archive of his research and ways to get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Considered, curious, playful. The user's explicit order of priorities is aesthetic
technical creator, interesting person, then rigorous researcher.

## References and Anti-references

- La Cabra and Onyx Coffee Lab: confident composition, imagery, and motion.
- Apple, OpenAI, and Anthropic: the user's preference for simplicity and spaciousness.
- Avoid scattered chunky background pixels, competing decorative styles, and
  generic AI landing-page flourishes.
- Keep the existing person and cat, including their scroll and rest interactions.
- Handwriting belongs in the name, signature, and faint background DripNowhy tags.
- Retain the original EB Garamond reading text and JetBrains Mono small text.
- Background is deliberately quiet: sparse DripNowhy tags in varied handwritten
  fonts and low opacity. Independently staggered 4.5–8.5 second cycles continually
  regenerate their positions, angles, and sizes (0.65–1.5×); use six slots on desktop
  and four on mobile. Tags stay still while visible, with soft fades and brief gaps.
  Avoid ripples, writhing contours, and attention-seeking
  motion; the user disliked the organic drifting forms.

## Design Principles

1. Personal details carry the identity: keep the person, cat, portrait, and signature.
2. Give motion a clear role and a controlled composition.
3. Let the surrounding interface be calm enough for the playful details to stand out.
4. Preserve the research content and direct access to papers, code, and CV.
5. Make the page feel considered on both desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain the existing keyboard navigation, theme choice, and reduced-motion support.
Keep body text readable and the reading column clear of decorative background art.
